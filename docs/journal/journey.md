# The Journey

This page is the story behind the system. It matters because Master Rulebook was not built by someone who started as an experienced trader; it was built by learning, losing, testing, automating, and gradually replacing improvisation with rules.

## Timeline

### September 2025 — The Beginning

- Started with ~$20K and zero experience
- Didn't understand puts or calls
- No system, no plan, just learning by doing

This was the pure tuition phase: real money spent learning how options even work.

### October-November 2025 — Discovery

- Learned options mechanics through trial and error (expensive)
- YouTube algorithm surfaced OVTLYR
- Watched Chris's videos extensively — learned the 3-layer framework (Market, Sector, Stock)
- Registered for OVTLYR platform late November
- Account bleeding: $20K grinding down toward $10K

This period mattered because it introduced the framework that later became Plan M and much of the system's market structure.

### December 2025 — The Commitment

- Account hit bottom: ~$9,650
- Fully committed to OVTLYR framework around Dec 10
- Started building automated screeners with Claude
- First rules-based trades

This is the turning point: moving from "trying things" to actually following a process.

### January 2026 — The System Takes Shape

- Plan H (H9 EMA21 pullback) formalized and backtested
- Plan M (OVTLYR swing) screeners automated
- Plan C (crash shorts) designed and coded
- Regime detection system built
- Account growing rapidly — system in its sweet spot (choppy market)

By this point, the pieces were becoming a real system rather than a collection of tactics.

### February 2026 — Peak and Pullback

- Account peaked at ~$88,000 (~812% from Dec bottom)
- Deviated from rules — gave back ~$18K
- Lesson reinforced: the system works, deviation doesn't
- Plan Alpha (mean reversion) researched and built
- Bottom formation pipeline designed

This month proved both sides of the thesis: the rules could compound fast, and breaking them could take money back just as fast.

### March 2026 — Maturity

- Account at ~$70K, still up ~625% from Dec low
- Playing with house money — original $20K long recovered
- Four active plans covering all regimes
- Full automation: screeners, monitors, dashboards, alerts
- Regime detection, convergence analysis, health monitoring
- Started building knowledge base (this wiki)

This is where Master Rulebook stops being "one trader having a hot streak" and starts looking like an actual operating system for decision-making.

## What Made It Work

These are the structural reasons the system worked, not just the sequence of dates.

1. **Found a framework** (OVTLYR) that matched how markets actually move
2. **Systematized it** — turned discretionary signals into rules-based execution
3. **Automated the boring parts** — screeners, alerts, health checks
4. **Backtested everything** — no live trade without historical validation
5. **Accepted the tuition** — the $10K lost learning was the price of the $78K gained executing
