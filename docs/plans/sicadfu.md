This is the system's cash-management plan for times when no other plan has an edge. It matters because "doing nothing" is not a failure state here; it is an explicit, rules-based choice.

The most common plan. When literally nothing else is set up.

## Rules

1. Buy shares of **SGOV** (0-3 month T-bills ETF)
2. Wait until a Plan M, Alpha, or ETF setup appears
3. **Monthly rollover:** Sell all SGOV on last trading day of month, repurchase on first trading day of next month

SGOV is a very short-term Treasury-bill ETF. In plain English, it is where idle cash sits so the account can earn yield while staying liquid.

## Why Monthly Rollover?

- Eliminates market risk over month-end
- Gets cash faster than waiting 5-7 days for dividend
- Allows buying more SGOV immediately

## Exit

A Plan M, Alpha, or ETF setup appears → sell SGOV, deploy capital.

!!! tip "Patience is a Position"
    SICADFU is not doing nothing. It's earning yield while staying ready. The system fires when conditions are right, not when you're bored.
